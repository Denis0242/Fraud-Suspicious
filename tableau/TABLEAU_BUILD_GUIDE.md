# Tableau Build Guide

## Project 3 — Final Approved Dashboard

### Visual standard
- White background with thin light-gray borders.
- Six KPI cards across the top.
- Exactly six visualizations in a 3 x 2 grid.
- Professional mixed colors.
- No stacked bar charts.
- Keep labels, legends, and values fully visible.
- Display month/year dates as MM/YY, for example 07/26.
- Date filter should also use MM/YY.

### KPI cards
1. Total Transactions
2. Fraud Alerts
3. Confirmed Fraud
4. Affected Customers
5. Fraudulent Amount
6. Detection Rate

### Final six visualizations
1. Transactions, Fraud Alerts & Confirmed Fraud Trend
   - One combined multi-line trend visualization.
   - Total Transactions, Fraud Alerts, and Confirmed Fraud.
   - X-axis formatted MM/YY.

2. Fraud Pattern Distribution
   - Donut chart.
   - Percentages inside the donut.
   - Clear legend to the right.

3. Top 5 Countries by Flagged Amount
   - Vertical bar chart.
   - Full country names and compact K/M labels.

4. Fraud Activity Heatmap (Day vs Hour)
   - Heatmap with alert counts printed inside each cell.

5. Transaction Amount vs Fraud Risk
   - Scatter plot.
   - Transaction amount on x-axis and fraud risk score on y-axis.

6. Alert Disposition
   - Distinct vertical bars.
   - Closed (Non-Fraud), Confirmed Fraud, Escalated for Review, Ongoing Investigation.

This is the final approved dashboard layout.
